1 · Introduction

KLiXO Coach is a web portal that lets a coaching centre run daily attendance and its class timetable from a phone or computer — no spreadsheets to touch.

Teachers open their classes for the day and tap each student Present, Absent or Late. Owners get a live dashboard (overall attendance, students who are falling behind, schedule problems) and full control of the centre's data: students, batches, enrollments, teachers, rooms, holidays and settings.

Two kinds of users

RoleWhat they can doWhere they land after signing in
TeacherSee and mark their own classes (today and past).Today
OwnerEverything a teacher can do, plus the dashboard and all centre management. May also teach.Dashboard
Note The portal works on any modern phone or laptop browser. There is nothing to install.

2 · Signing in

Open the portal address and sign in with your phone number and PIN. Your PIN is set by the centre owner.

  1. Go to the portal address (e.g. klixo-coach.vercel.app).
  2. Type your registered phone number.
  3. Type your PIN.
  4. Tap Sign in.
Locked out? After several wrong PINs in a row, sign-in is paused for about 15 minutes to keep the account safe. Wait and try again, or ask the owner to reset your PIN.
Tip Sessions stay signed in for 12 hours, then ask for the PIN again. Use Sign out (top-right) on a shared device.

3 · Getting around

The bar at the top is the same on every screen. Teachers see Today. Owners also see Dashboard, Timetable and Manage. Your name and role sit on the right, next to Sign out.

Note Owner-only screens are hidden from teachers, and are protected on the server too — a teacher cannot reach them by typing the address.

4 · Teacher — Today

Your home screen. It lists the classes you teach for the selected day, with how many students are present out of the roster.

  • Each card shows the batch, time, room, and a marked / unmarked status.
  • Use the date arrows to look at earlier days. Future days can't be marked.
  • Tap a class to open its roster and mark attendance.
Note You only see classes assigned to you (including any you are covering as a substitute). The owner sees every class.

5 · Marking attendance

Open a class to see its students. Set each one to Present, Absent or Late, then save.

  1. Tap the status for each student (Present / Absent / Late).
  2. Tap Save at the bottom.
Tip The roster only lists students whose enrollment had started by that date — late joiners don't appear on earlier classes, so percentages stay fair.
Can't mark? Cancelled classes and future dates can't be marked.

6 · Correcting past attendance

Open any past class and change a student's status. The portal records how each mark was made so corrections are transparent.

SituationWhat happens
Marking today, first timeSaved as a normal app mark — no reason needed.
Filling in a class from the past that was never markedSaved as a manual backfill with one shared reason.
Changing a student's already-saved statusSaved as a manual correction — that student needs its own reason.
A student you didn't changeLeft exactly as it was (no new record).
Note Every manual mark and its reason appears in the owner's Manual-mark audit on the dashboard.

7 · Owner — Dashboard

The owner's home screen: a live read of attendance across the centre, plus health checks for the schedule and data.

  • Top tiles — overall attendance %, marks recorded, students tracked, and how many are below the threshold.
  • Schedule health — a banner flagging timetable clashes (tap through to the Timetable).
  • Data integrity — a banner if a direct spreadsheet edit left a broken reference (e.g. a class pointing at a deleted room).
  • Attendance by batch and Defaulters — who is falling below the threshold.
  • Manual-mark audit — every correction, who made it, when and why.

8 · Owner — Today & extra classes

The owner's Today screen lists all classes for the day. You can also schedule a one-off extra class that isn't part of the weekly timetable.

Add an extra class

  1. Choose the batch, date, time, room and teacher.
  2. Save. If the room or teacher is already booked at that time, the portal blocks it and tells you what clashes.
Note A student double-booking is allowed but warns you; a room or teacher double-booking is blocked.

9 · Timetable

The weekly plan. Each rule (a batch on certain weekdays at a time, in a room, with a teacher) generates the actual dated classes automatically.

Add or edit a rule

  1. Pick the batch, the weekdays, start/end time, room and teacher.
  2. Set Effective from (and optionally Effective to).
  3. Save — classes are generated for the next 30 days.
Editing is safe Changing a rule regenerates only future, unmarked classes. Past classes and any class that already has attendance are frozen, and substitutes you set are kept.
Generation Classes are also re-generated automatically every night, so the next 30 days are always populated.

10 · Manage hub

One place for all centre data. Each card opens a manager and shows a live count.

Note Everything here is owner-only. Changes save straight to the centre's records — there is no separate spreadsheet step.

11 · Students

Add students, edit their details, and open a profile with their batches and full attendance history.

Add a student

  1. Open Students → + Add student.
  2. Enter the name and join date (phone numbers are optional).
  3. Save, then open the profile to enroll them in batches.

Profile, history & enrollments

  • Attendance — present-of-total and a colour-coded percentage.
  • Enrollments — current and past batches; End stops an enrollment from a date.
  • Add to batch — enroll into another batch (warns if it clashes with an existing one).
  • Deactivate — removes them from future rosters while keeping all history.
Privacy Phone numbers are visible only here, to owners. Teachers never see student phone numbers.

12 · Batches & enrollments

A batch is a class group (subject, level, fee) with a default teacher and room. Enroll students from either the batch or the student side.

Create a batch

Manage a batch & its students

  1. Edit the batch name, subject, teacher, room, fee and level at the top.
  2. Under Students, use Enroll student to add one (choose a start date).
  3. Use End next to a student to withdraw them from a date.
  4. Deactivate hides the batch from new rules and pickers; existing classes stay.
Tip A withdrawn student still appears on the rosters for the days they were enrolled, so past attendance stays correct.

13 · Teachers & PINs

Add staff, set roles, reset PINs and activate/deactivate accounts.

Add a teacher

  1. Enter name, phone, role (Teacher or Owner) and a 4–6 digit PIN.
  2. Save. They can now sign in with that phone + PIN.

Edit, reset PIN, deactivate

  • Reset PIN — set a new 4–6 digit PIN if a teacher is locked out or forgot it.
  • Deactivate — stops sign-in while keeping records.
Last owner is protected You can't deactivate or demote the only remaining active owner — that would lock the centre out.
Note A phone number can belong to only one teacher.

14 · Rooms

Manage the rooms classes are held in, and their capacity.

Delete is guarded A room can't be deleted while an active batch, a timetable rule, or a future class still uses it. Reassign those first.

15 · Holidays

Mark dates the centre is closed. Generated classes on those dates are removed automatically.

  1. Pick a date, type a name, tap + Add.
  2. The schedule re-generates: future unmarked classes on that day are removed.
  3. Removing a holiday puts those classes back.
Note Classes that already have attendance are never removed by a holiday change.

17 · How attendance % works

  • Each class generates from the timetable. A rule (batch + weekdays + time + room + teacher) creates dated classes for 30 days ahead.
  • The roster is enrollment-aware. A student counts for a class only if their enrollment covers that date — late joiners and withdrawn students are handled automatically.
  • Percentage = present ÷ fully-marked classes. Unmarked classes don't count against anyone. The latest saved mark per class is the one that counts.
  • Clash rules. Same room or same teacher overlapping = blocked. Same student in two overlapping classes = warning.

18 · Security & data

  • Sign-in: phone + PIN; sessions last 12 hours; repeated wrong PINs pause sign-in for ~15 minutes.
  • Roles: owner-only screens are enforced on the server, not just hidden.
  • Privacy: student phone numbers are owner-only; teachers see names only.
  • Integrity: the dashboard flags broken references if records are edited outside the portal.
  • Records are kept: deactivating a student, teacher or batch never deletes their history.

19 · Troubleshooting & FAQ

Question / problemAnswer
"Invalid phone or PIN."Check the phone number is the registered one and the PIN is correct. Ask the owner to reset the PIN if needed.
"Too many attempts."Wait ~15 minutes, or have the owner reset the PIN.
A class won't let me mark itIt's cancelled, or it's a future date. Past and current classes can be marked.
A student isn't on the rosterTheir enrollment may start after that date, may have ended, or they're deactivated.
"Clash" when saving a class/ruleThe room or teacher is already booked at that time. Change the time, room or teacher.
Can't delete a roomIt's still used by a batch, rule, or future class. Reassign those first.
Dashboard shows an integrity warningA record points at something deleted (often from a manual spreadsheet edit). Fix the reference in the relevant manager.

20 · Glossary

TermMeaning

Session / classA single dated class, generated from a rule or added as an extra.
DefaulterA student below the attendance threshold.
BackfillMarking a past class that was never marked.
Manual markA correction or backfill, recorded with a reason.
SubstituteA different teacher covering a single class.
